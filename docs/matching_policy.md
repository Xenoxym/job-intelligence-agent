# Matching policy

Deterministic, versioned rules combine skill coverage with evidenced responsibility clusters,
role family, geography, freshness and evidence strength. Company relevance defaults neutral
unless explicitly preferred. Skills are extracted with word boundaries and aliases.
Required and preferred lines are separated; missing preferred skills are not disqualifiers.
Years and Senior/Staff/Principal labels create explicit uncertainty/gaps, not rejection.
No verified employment tenure exists in the initial profile.

Scores expose positive dimensions on 0–100 and gap dimensions where higher means more gap.
Recommendations are APPLY, REVIEW, LOW PRIORITY, SKIP. Unknown location, empty descriptions,
or explicit authorization/clearance requirements need review. Never infer legal eligibility.

Feedback uses the latest observed outcome per job, preventing repeated updates from gaming
the prior. Role-family and work-mode priors are smoothed and total adjustment is capped.
Applied is neutral; OA/recruiter/interview/offer are positive; rejection is modestly negative.
Snapshots expose base score, adjustment and the observations driving it. Outcome correlation
is not causal and does not prove employer preference. Demo feedback is isolated from real jobs.
